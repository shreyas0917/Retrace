# Time Off FAQ

## How much paid time off do I get?

Full-time staff earn 1.5 days of paid time off per month, which adds up to 18 days per year. Time off is shown in the HR portal under "Balances".

## Can I carry days over?

Up to 10 unused days roll over into the next year. Any balance above 10 days expires on January 31.

## How far ahead should I ask?

Please give your manager at least 2 weeks' notice for time off longer than three days.
