# Refund FAQ

## How long do I have to return something?

Customers can return physical products for a full refund within 60 days of delivery. Items must include all accessories. Opened items may be subject to a 15% restocking fee.

## Digital purchases

Digital purchases can be refunded within 30 days if the customer contacts the sales team.

## How is the refund paid?

Refunds are issued as store credit by default. A refund to the original card can be requested and takes up to 21 days.
