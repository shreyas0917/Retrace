# Procurement Policy

## Purpose

This policy governs how the company buys goods and services. It exists to control spend, manage vendor risk, and ensure that purchases are documented. All amounts refer to the total contract value over the full term, including renewals.

## Approval Thresholds

- Purchases under $1,000 may be made by a manager using a corporate purchasing card without a requisition, subject to the Expense Reimbursement Policy for receipts and reconciliation.
- Purchases from $1,000 to $10,000 require Form PR-1 (Purchase Requisition) and at least 2 written quotes.
- Purchases above $10,000 require 3 competitive bids and sign-off from the Finance Director.
- Purchases above $50,000 additionally require CFO approval and a Legal review of the contract.

Splitting a purchase into smaller orders to stay below a threshold is prohibited.

## Vendor Onboarding

New vendors are onboarded by the Procurement team before a purchase order is issued. The vendor must provide a tax form (W-9 or local equivalent), banking details on company letterhead, and a signed copy of the vendor code of conduct. Vendors whose products process customer data must also complete the vendor security questionnaire and be approved by the Security team before any data is shared, consistent with the Security Policy. Vendor onboarding typically takes 10 business days.

## Software and SaaS

All software purchases, including free tiers and trials that store company data, must be reviewed by IT through an IT-3 ticket as required by the IT Acceptable Use Policy. Procurement maintains a preferred vendor list; purchasing from a preferred vendor does not remove the quote requirements above but may shorten onboarding. Auto-renewing contracts must be entered in the contract register with a reminder 60 days before renewal.

## Purchase Orders and Payment

A purchase order (PO) is required for any purchase of $1,000 or more. Vendors must reference the PO number on invoices; invoices without a PO are returned. Standard payment terms are net 30 days from receipt of a valid invoice. Early payment discounts may be accepted when they exceed 2%. Invoices are submitted to ap@example.com.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any personal or financial relationship with a vendor using the Conflict of Interest form before participating in a purchase decision. Gifts from vendors with a value above $50 must be declined or reported to Procurement.

## Contracts

Only officers of the company and employees with delegated signing authority may sign contracts. Standard contract templates are maintained by Legal. Any deviation from the standard limitation of liability or data protection terms requires Legal approval regardless of contract value.

## Receiving and Disputes

The requester confirms receipt of goods or services in the procurement system before payment is released. Disputes with vendors are raised through Procurement, which coordinates with Legal where necessary.

## Record Keeping

Procurement records, including quotes, bids, and signed contracts, are retained for 7 years after the contract ends, in line with the Data Retention Policy for financial records.
