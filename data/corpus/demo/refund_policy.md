# Refund Policy

## Eligibility

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase if the product is unused and in original packaging.
Digital goods are eligible for refund only within 14 days and only if the download was never completed.

## Process

1. Submit a refund request through the support portal.
2. Include the order ID and reason for the refund.
3. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

Chargebacks initiated without contacting support first may result in account suspension.
