# IT Acceptable Use Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to everyone who uses company systems, networks, or data, including employees, contractors, and interns. It complements the Security Policy, which governs production access and secrets handling.

## Accounts and Passwords

Passwords for company accounts must be at least 14 characters long and must not reuse any of the previous 8 passwords. Passwords must be rotated every 180 days, or immediately if compromise is suspected. All passwords must be stored in the approved password manager, Vaultkeeper. Writing passwords on paper or storing them in plain text files is prohibited.

Multi-factor authentication is required for email, the VPN, the HR portal, and all production systems, consistent with the Security Policy. Shared accounts are not permitted; every login must be attributable to one person.

## Devices

Company laptops are managed by the IT device management profile and must not be unenrolled. Screens must lock automatically after 10 minutes of inactivity. Personal phones may access company email only after enrolling in the mobile device management (MDM) program, which allows IT to wipe company data if the device is lost.

Removable USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops. Files may be shared only through the approved cloud drive. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to helpdesk@example.com within 4 hours of discovery so that remote wipe can be triggered.

## Software

Only software from the approved catalog may be installed. Requests for new software are submitted as an IT-3 ticket (Software Request) and are reviewed by IT within 3 business days. Software that processes customer data also requires a security review through the Procurement Policy vendor process. Browser extensions must come from the approved list.

## Network Use

The corporate VPN must be used when accessing internal systems from outside the office. Guests must use the Guest Wi-Fi network, which is isolated from internal resources. Peer-to-peer file sharing, cryptocurrency mining, and running personal servers on company equipment are prohibited.

## Email and Messaging

Company email is for business use; limited personal use is acceptable if it does not interfere with work. Auto-forwarding company email to personal accounts is disabled and must not be circumvented. Suspected phishing messages should be reported using the "Report Phish" button, which forwards them to the security team. Customer data must never be sent over personal messaging applications.

## Monitoring and Privacy

The company logs network activity, authentication events, and device health for security purposes. Logs are retained according to the Data Retention Policy. Users should have no expectation of privacy in company systems, although personal content is not reviewed without approval from HR and Legal.

## Violations

Violations are reported to the IT help desk or to security@example.com. Consequences range from retraining to termination depending on severity. Deliberately bypassing security controls is treated as a serious violation.
