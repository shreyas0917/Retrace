# Travel Policy

## Scope

This policy covers business travel booking, class of service, and lodging. Meal per diems, mileage, and receipt requirements are set in the Expense Reimbursement Policy and are not repeated here, except that the daily meal per diem of $65 domestic and $95 international also applies to travel days.

## Booking

All travel must be booked through the corporate travel portal, TripDesk, so that trips are visible to Security and insured through the corporate card program. Flights and hotels should be booked at least 14 days in advance; bookings made less than 14 days before departure require manager approval in the portal and a short reason. Travel booked outside TripDesk is reimbursed only in emergencies.

## Air Travel

Economy class is the standard for all flights. Business class is permitted for flights with a scheduled duration of more than 6 hours, with VP approval recorded in TripDesk. Premium economy may be chosen for flights over 4 hours without additional approval when the fare difference is under $200. Employees may keep frequent flyer points earned on business travel but must not choose a more expensive fare to earn them.

Travelers should select the lowest logical fare, defined as the lowest fare that departs within 2 hours of the preferred time and has at most one connection.

## Lodging

Hotel rates are capped at $250 per night for domestic travel and $325 per night in international and designated high-cost cities. The portal flags bookings above the cap for manager approval. Employees may stay with friends or family and claim a flat $50 per night in lieu of a hotel. Minibar, movies, and spa charges are personal expenses.

## Ground Transportation

Rental cars should be intermediate class or smaller. In the United States, employees must decline the rental company's insurance because coverage is provided by the corporate card program; outside the United States, basic insurance should be accepted. Ride-share and taxis are permitted when cheaper than a rental or when driving is impractical. Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at the rate in the Expense Reimbursement Policy.

## International Travel

International trips must be registered at least 10 business days before departure using Form TR-5 (International Travel Registration). Security reviews the destination and may require a loaner laptop for high-risk countries. Employees must carry only the data needed for the trip. Vaccination and visa costs are reimbursable.

## Combining Personal and Business Travel

Personal days may be added to a business trip if the total cost to the company does not increase. The employee pays the difference for any additional nights or fare changes and documents the comparison fare at booking time.

## Safety

Travelers must share their itinerary with their manager and keep the TripDesk mobile app installed so that the travel desk can reach them during emergencies. Lost passports should be reported to the travel desk and the local embassy immediately.

## Cancellations

Non-refundable bookings that must be cancelled for business reasons are reimbursable. Cancellations for personal reasons are reimbursable only when the trip was cancelled by the company.
