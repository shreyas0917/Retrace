# Benefits Overview

## Eligibility and Enrollment

Full-time employees are eligible for benefits from their first day. New hires must enroll within 31 days of their start date; otherwise enrollment waits for the annual open enrollment period, which runs from November 1 to November 15 each year. Changes outside those windows are allowed only for qualifying life events such as marriage, birth, or loss of other coverage, and must be submitted within 30 days of the event.

## Health Coverage

The company offers medical, dental, and vision plans. The company pays 85% of the premium for employee coverage and 60% of the premium for dependents. Two medical plans are available: a PPO plan with a $500 deductible and a high-deductible plan with a $1,600 deductible paired with a health savings account. The company contributes $750 per year to the health savings account for employees on the high-deductible plan.

## Retirement

The 401(k) plan matches 100% of employee contributions up to 4% of salary. Employees are automatically enrolled at a 4% contribution rate and may change it at any time. Company matching contributions vest gradually over 3 years: one third after each year of service. Employee contributions are always fully vested.

## Time Away

Paid time off accrues as described in the Employee Handbook, 1.25 days per month for a total of 15 days per year. In addition, employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year, which does not carry over, and 5 days of paid bereavement leave per qualifying event. The company observes 11 public holidays per year.

Parental leave is 16 weeks fully paid for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks fully paid for the secondary caregiver, available after 6 months of employment. Leave may be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement.

Employees with 5 years of continuous service may take a 4-week paid sabbatical, which must be scheduled at least 3 months in advance.

## Insurance

Basic life insurance of 2 times annual salary is provided at no cost, up to a maximum of $500,000. Employees may purchase additional coverage. Short-term disability pays 60% of salary for up to 12 weeks; long-term disability continues at 60% after that.

## Learning and Wellness

Each employee has a learning stipend of $1,200 per calendar year for courses, books, and conferences, approved by the manager and claimed through the Expense Portal. A separate wellness stipend of $300 per year covers gym memberships, fitness equipment, or mental health apps. Unused stipend amounts do not roll over.

The Employee Assistance Program offers 6 free confidential counseling sessions per issue per year for employees and household members. Call the number on the benefits card to schedule.

## Commuter and Other Benefits

Employees may set aside pre-tax funds for transit and parking. The company offers a $200 subsidy toward an annual transit pass. Employee referrals that result in a hire earn a $2,000 bonus, paid after the new hire completes 90 days.
