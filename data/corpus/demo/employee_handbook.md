# Employee Handbook

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 1.25 days of PTO per month, for a total of 15 days per year.
Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis according to scheduled hours.

Unused PTO may be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year.
Requests for PTO must be submitted at least 7 days in advance except for emergencies.

## Work Hours

Standard office hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 to 17:30 local time.
Remote work is permitted up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
