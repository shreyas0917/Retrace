# Remote Work Policy

## Purpose and Scope

This policy describes how employees may work away from a company office. It supplements the Employee Handbook, which sets the default allowance of up to 2 remote days per week with manager approval. Nothing in this document changes the standard office hours of 9:00 to 17:30 local time described in the handbook.

## Eligibility

Employees become eligible for regular remote work after completing 90 days of continuous employment and a satisfactory probation review. Roles that require physical presence, such as facilities, reception, and lab operations, are excluded from regular remote work but may request occasional remote days for specific circumstances.

Department heads may designate a role as remote-eligible. Employees in remote-eligible roles may work a third remote day each week by submitting Form RW-2 (Extended Remote Schedule) for approval by both the manager and the department head. Fully remote arrangements are approved only by the VP of People and are reviewed every 12 months.

## Requesting Remote Work

Recurring remote schedules must be requested using Form RW-1 (Remote Work Agreement) in the HR portal. Ad hoc remote days do not require a form but must be agreed with the manager the day before, except in emergencies. Managers must respond to Form RW-1 requests within 5 business days.

## Core Hours and Availability

Remote employees must be reachable during core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 in their team's primary time zone. Calendars must be kept current, and a status must be set in the chat tool when stepping away for more than 30 minutes. Remote work is not a substitute for PTO or sick leave; employees who are unwell should record sick leave rather than working remotely.

## Location Requirements

Remote work must normally be performed inside the employee's country of employment. Working from another country is permitted for up to 30 calendar days per year and requires approval at least 21 days in advance using Form RW-3 (Temporary International Work). Requests are reviewed by HR for tax and legal exposure and by Security for data access restrictions. Working from a sanctioned country is never permitted.

## Equipment and Stipends

The company provides a laptop, headset, and monitor to every employee. Remote workers may claim a one-time home office stipend of $400 for a desk, chair, or lighting, and a recurring home internet reimbursement of $50 per month. Both are submitted through the Expense Portal under the category "Remote Work" and follow the Expense Reimbursement Policy, including the 45-day submission deadline.

Company equipment must be used on a company-managed device profile. Personal printers may not be used for documents classified Confidential or above.

## Security Expectations

Remote employees must connect through the corporate VPN for any access to internal systems, use the screen lock settings enforced by the IT Acceptable Use Policy, and never work on Confidential material in public spaces where screens can be observed. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT help desk within 4 hours.

## Review and Termination of Arrangements

Managers may suspend a remote arrangement with 2 weeks' written notice if performance or collaboration concerns arise. Employees may end an arrangement at any time. HR reviews all Form RW-1 agreements annually in October.
