# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Overview

Employees are reimbursed for reasonable business expenses that are necessary to perform their jobs. All claims are submitted through the Expense Portal. This policy covers general expenses; travel-specific rules such as flight class and hotel caps are in the Travel Policy, and remote work stipends are in the Remote Work Policy.

## Submission Deadlines

Expense reports must be submitted within 45 days of the date the expense was incurred. Reports submitted after 45 days require written justification and VP approval, and reports older than 90 days will not be reimbursed. Corporate card transactions must be reconciled in the Expense Portal by the 5th of the following month.

## Receipts and Documentation

An itemized receipt is required for any single expense of $25 or more. For expenses under $25, a receipt is preferred but not required if the business purpose is clearly described. Credit card statements alone are not accepted as receipts. Lost receipts must be declared using Form EXP-7 (Missing Receipt Declaration), which may be used no more than 3 times per calendar year.

## Approval Thresholds

- Expense reports up to $2,500 are approved by the employee's direct manager.
- Reports above $2,500 also require approval from the department VP.
- Any single item above $10,000 is routed to Finance for review before payment.

Managers may not approve their own expenses. Expenses for a manager are approved by that manager's own manager.

## Meals and Entertainment

Daily meal per diem while traveling is $65 for domestic travel and $95 for international travel. Per diem covers all meals and incidentals; separate meal receipts are not needed when per diem is claimed. Client entertainment is reimbursable with the names of attendees and the business purpose recorded. Alcohol is not reimbursable except as part of a client dinner, where it is capped at $40 per person.

## Mileage and Ground Transport

Use of a personal vehicle for business is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile, which covers fuel, wear, and insurance. Commuting between home and the regular office is not reimbursable. Parking fees and tolls incurred on business trips are reimbursable with receipts. Ride-share and taxi fares are reimbursable when public transport is impractical.

## Non-Reimbursable Items

The following are never reimbursed: traffic fines, personal entertainment, minibar charges, spa or gym fees, upgrades not permitted by the Travel Policy, and purchases for family members. Gift cards may only be purchased through the Procurement team.

## Payment Timing

Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle, normally within 10 business days of final approval. Reimbursements are paid to the bank account on file in the HR portal. Finance may hold payment for reports that are missing receipts or business purpose.

## Audit and Compliance

Finance audits a random sample of 10% of expense reports each quarter. Falsifying expense claims is grounds for immediate termination. Questions about this policy go to expenses@example.com.
