# New Hire Onboarding Checklist

## Before the Start Date

The hiring manager owns pre-boarding. At least 5 business days before the start date the manager must submit Form IT-1 (Access Request) listing the systems the new hire needs, and Form IT-2 (Equipment Request) for a laptop and peripherals. Laptops are shipped to remote hires so that they arrive 2 business days before day one.

HR sends the welcome packet 1 week before the start date. It contains the offer confirmation, the Employee Handbook, the tax forms, and instructions for the HR portal. The manager assigns an onboarding buddy from the same team before day one.

## Day One

- Collect the ID badge from reception or, for remote hires, complete the virtual identity verification call.
- Sign in to the laptop and enroll in the device management profile.
- Set up the password manager and multi-factor authentication as described in the IT Acceptable Use Policy.
- Attend the new hire welcome session at 10:00.
- Meet the onboarding buddy and the manager for a 30-minute introduction.

## First 3 Days

Complete the Security Awareness training in the learning portal within 3 days of starting. Access to production systems is not granted until this training is complete. Submit Form HR-1 (Direct Deposit) in the HR portal so the first paycheck is not delayed.

## First 2 Weeks

Mandatory compliance training, which covers the code of conduct, anti-harassment, and data protection, must be completed within 14 days of the start date. New hires read the Security Policy, the Data Retention Policy, and the Expense Reimbursement Policy and confirm in the HR portal that they have done so.

## Benefits Enrollment

New hires have 31 days from their start date to enroll in health, dental, and vision coverage. Missing the window means waiting until the next open enrollment in November unless a qualifying life event occurs. Retirement plan enrollment is automatic at a 4% contribution unless the employee changes it.

## Probation and Check-Ins

The probation period is 90 days. Managers hold structured check-ins at 30, 60, and 90 days using the Check-In template in the performance tool. The 90-day check-in doubles as the probation review; a satisfactory review unlocks eligibility for regular remote work under the Remote Work Policy.

## Role-Specific Steps

Engineers are added to the on-call shadow rotation after 60 days and complete incident response training before shadowing. Customer-facing staff complete the product certification within 30 days. Managers complete the manager fundamentals course within 60 days.

## Offboarding Note

The same forms are used in reverse when someone leaves: the manager submits IT-1 to remove access on the last day, and equipment is returned within 10 business days using the prepaid return label.

## Contacts

Questions about onboarding go to onboarding@example.com. Equipment issues go to the IT help desk.
